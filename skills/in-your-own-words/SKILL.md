# In Your Own Words

Verify alignment on user intent and requirements before executing complex tasks.
Playback the core objectives in your own words to prevent misunderstandings and misaligned effort.

## Playback Structure

Structure intent playbacks cleanly.
Goals and problems are stated clearly before proposing or executing implementations.
Use the following explicit structure:

1. **Goal.** State the intended outcome from the perspective of the user or consumer.
2. **Problem.** Define the defect, friction, or technical limitation being resolved.
3. **Scope and non-goals.** Clarify the boundaries of what will and will not change.
4. **Proposed implementation.** Outline the specific sequence of edits and verification steps.

## When to Use

Apply this playback structure in the following situations:

- Starting a multi-phase or cross-cutting feature request.
- Receiving complex instructions with multiple competing requirements.
- Taking over a task from another agent or session transcript.
- Resolving ambiguous user feedback or architectural forks.

## Execution Rules

Follow these principles when playing back intent:

- Use plain declarative prose.
- Do not echo the user prompt verbatim.
- Identify unstated assumptions and surface them explicitly.
- Request confirmation only when genuine product decisions cannot be resolved through empirical probes.
